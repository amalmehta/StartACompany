What the site is

Start a Company turns your answers to a few questions into a general checklist of the paperwork for starting a US company, with state fees, deadlines and links to official government sites. It is free to use. You don't need an account.

Not legal or tax advice

The checklist is general information. It is not legal, tax, accounting or financial advice, and using the site doesn't create a lawyer–client or any other professional relationship. The site doesn't review your situation, doesn't file anything for you and doesn't act on your behalf. Laws, fees and deadlines change, and your situation may have details the questions don't cover. Before you file or decide anything important, confirm with the official source linked from each step and, where it matters, with a licensed lawyer or tax professional in your state.

Accuracy

Fees were checked against public sources on the review date shown on each page. They can go out of date at any time, and some states add surcharges, expedite fees or taxes that scale with revenue or shares. The site is provided “as is”, without warranties of any kind, including accuracy, completeness or fitness for a particular purpose.

How the site is paid for

The site is free for founders. Some pages may show links to paid company-formation services (for example doola, Firstbase or LegalZoom). Those links are marked “referral”. If you buy through one, the service may pay Start a Company a referral fee. That fee comes from the service, not from you.

Referral services only appear where they fit your answers, and the official do-it-yourself filing link always comes first, because filing yourself costs only the state fee. A service may also be listed with “no referral” when it has no referral program; the site earns nothing from those. Referral fees never change the fees, steps or recommendations the checklist shows.

Your purchase is between you and that service, under its own terms and privacy policy. Start a Company isn't responsible for third-party services.
