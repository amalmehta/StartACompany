Privacy

The short version: your answers stay in your browser. The site has no accounts, no analytics and no server of its own.

Template. This page is a starting draft and has not been reviewed by a lawyer. Have it reviewed before relying on it. Last updated 2026-10-22.

What the site stores, and where

One entry in your browser's local storage, named start-a-company:v1. It never leaves your device unless you export it. It holds:

  • Your answers: state, structure (LLC, C corp, S corp, sole proprietor or not sure), where to incorporate, number of founders, whether you'll pay contractors or employees, and yes/no answers to raising venture money, expecting $80k+ profit, selling taxable goods, a licensed industry, running it from home, and founders living outside the US.
  • Your progress: which checklist steps you've ticked as done.
  • Your settings: theme, whether to show costs, hide finished steps, and show step details by default.

Which steps you've expanded is kept in memory only and is forgotten when you close the page. The site sets no cookies of its own. If your browser blocks storage, the site still works but forgets your answers when you leave.

To delete everything, open Settings and choose Reset all, or clear this site's data in your browser.

What the site never collects

No names, emails, accounts, payment details, analytics, tracking pixels or advertising cookies. Your answers are not sent to the site's owner, to referral partners or to anyone else.

Other services involved

  • Hosting. The site is served by GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses for security. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Fonts. Fonts load from Google Fonts, so your browser sends Google its IP address and user agent when the page loads.
  • Feedback. The Feedback tab opens a prefilled GitHub issue in a new tab: your text and, if you leave the box ticked, your state and structure. Nothing is sent until you post it on GitHub, where it is public.
  • Export and import. Export saves a JSON file of the stored entry to your device. Import reads a file you choose. Neither uploads anything.
  • Links you click. Official sites and any referral service you open have their own privacy policies. A referral link tells that service you came from Start a Company, and the service may set its own cookies to track the referral. Your answers are not included.
  • State pages. The “Build my checklist” button on a state page adds the state's two-letter code to the address (for example ?state=DE) so the plan starts with that state. The code is removed from the address once the page loads.
