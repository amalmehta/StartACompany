Forming a company in New Jersey

What New Jersey charges to form an LLC or corporation, what you file each year, and where to file it. Fees last reviewed 2026-10-11.

Fees at a glance

New Jersey state fees
LLC to form$125
LLC to keep each year$75 a year
Corporation to form$125
Corporation to keep each year$575 a year
Report dueBy the end of your formation anniversary month
Registering an out-of-state company here$125 (LLC), $125 (corporation)

Yearly cost is the report fee plus any minimum tax owed no matter how much you earn. Taxes that grow with revenue or shares aren't included.

Corporations owe the Corporation Business Tax minimum every year ($500–$2,000 by revenue for C corps, $375–$1,500 for S corps); the lowest tier is included above. Multi-owner LLCs with NJ income pay $150 per owner each year.

Filings for an LLC

  • File Articles of Organization with New Jersey $125 state fee
    This is the filing that creates the company. File online with the New Jersey business filing office. Corporations owe the Corporation Business Tax minimum every year ($500–$2,000 by revenue for C corps, $375–$1,500 for S corps); the lowest tier is included above. Multi-owner LLCs with NJ income pay $150 per owner each year.
  • New Jersey annual report $75/yr
    By the end of your formation anniversary month. If you miss it, the state can dissolve the company.
